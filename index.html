🫧Always boil tap water for at least 1 minute before drinking if you see turbidity alerts in your area.
🤒If multiple family members develop diarrhea, vomiting, or fever — report immediately and visit your nearest health center.
🧪Water that looks clear can still be contaminated. Trust the CPCB quality readings, not just appearance.
